Fixed-price remote work

Send the brief. A small studio will reply with a price and a written scope, or tell you we can’t take it.

Kindbrief handles a short list of remote jobs: product listings, process write-ups from a screen recording, bilingual subtitles, written code reviews, and App Store listing copy. We are not a bidding board, and we don’t keep a public list of freelancers.

If the job fits a package below, the price is already set. If it doesn’t, describe it anyway. We’ll say whether we can do it, and at what price, before any work starts.

First-order credit: A$20 off. Your first paid order of A$40 or more gets A$20 off. One credit per customer. We’ll honour it, and we’ll show it on the quote. It isn’t cash and you can’t transfer it.

Packages

Prices are in Australian dollars. US dollar figures are approximate equivalents on our standing grid (A$20 = US$15, A$40 = US$25), not a live exchange rate. Where we compare a price with another published listing, the source is linked. None of these packages is one-tenth of the cited price.

English product listing

A$20 (about US$15) · 2 business days · 1 revision

One product, for Amazon or Shopify. You get a title, five bullet points, a product description, and backend search terms or tag suggestions.

  • Included: one English listing, written from the facts you provide.
  • Not included: photography, A+ module design, account access, ads, or any promise of rank or sales.

Process write-up from a screen recording

A$40 (about US$25) · 3 business days · 2 revisions

One recording, up to 15 minutes. You get a step-by-step procedure a new team member can follow, with screenshots from the video, a short “done” checklist, and notes on any unclear parts. Delivered as a Google Doc or a Word file.

  • Included: one process, from one Loom, Zoom, or similar screen recording (or a voice note of the same length).
  • Not included: legal, medical, or safety-critical procedures; recordings that contain passwords or payment details; more than one process in this price.

GoodAiDeas lists a similar job — one SOP from a screen recording up to 20 minutes, with screenshots and one revision — at a fixed US$79. Ours is 15 minutes rather than 20, at about US$25, which is about 68% under that published price (roughly one-third of it, not one-tenth). Source: Convert a Loom Video to an SOP, GoodAiDeas.

Bilingual subtitles, English plus Chinese

A$40 (about US$25) per 10 minutes of video · 3 business days · 2 revisions

English captions, timed, plus a Simplified or Traditional Chinese translation on the same timeline. You get SRT and VTT files. Videos shorter than 10 minutes are charged as 10 minutes; longer videos add another 10-minute block.

Written code review

A$40 (about US$25) · 3 business days · 2 written follow-ups

A written review of a personal repository or a practice take-home, up to about 1,000 lines. You get findings on readability, likely bugs, tests, and structure, with suggestions in priority order. We can also note how you might talk about the project in an interview.

  • Included: a written review. Languages we already take: Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, common web projects, and iOS/Swift. Other languages are possible if we see a sample first.
  • Not included: rewriting the project, opening a pull request, or writing, completing, or submitting coursework, exams, or hiring take-home tests. If it is assessed work, you change and submit the code yourself.

How it works

  1. Send the form. Pick a package or describe the job, and include a deadline if you have one.
  2. We reply by email with a written scope and a fixed price, or we decline. There is no fee for a declined request.
  3. If you accept, you pay the quoted amount. The first-order credit is taken off at this point when it applies. Then we do the work and send it back.

Payment is by card, through a payment link we send after you accept the quote. If that link isn’t ready yet, we’ll say so in the reply and we won’t ask you to pay until it is.

FAQ

Who does the work?

A small studio. We are not a marketplace with a bench of named freelancers. If we need a contractor for part of a job, the quote and the delivery still come from us.

Do you have reviews on this site?

Not yet. We won’t invent reviews, ratings, or client stories.

How does the A$20 credit work?

It applies to your first paid order of A$40 or more, once, for one customer. We’ll show it on the quote. It doesn’t apply to the A$20 product-listing package on its own, because that job is already A$20. It isn’t cash, and you can’t transfer it to someone else.

How do I pay?

After you accept a written quote, we send a card payment link for that amount. We don’t take payment before the scope is agreed. If our payment account is still being set up, we’ll tell you in the reply rather than invent another method.

What won’t you take?

In-person local tasks. Writing, completing, or submitting schoolwork, exams, or hiring take-home tests. Videos you don’t have the right to use. Promises of sales, search rank, or app-store placement. Overnight staffing, or a public directory of freelancers.

What currency are the prices in?

Australian dollars. We are not GST-registered, so GST is not added. US dollar amounts are approximate equivalents, not a live conversion.

How do I contact you?

Use the form on this page. We don’t publish a phone number.
